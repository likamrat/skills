SYNTHETIC EVAL FIXTURE

Interviewer: Walk me through a claim from arrival to payment.

Claims lead: A claim reaches a shared inbox. One coordinator opens the email and copies the policy number, claimant name, and amount into the claims workbook. The coordinator sends medical claims to a specialist. Property claims under $5,000 usually stay with the general queue.

Interviewer: Is that threshold always used?

Claims lead: Not exactly. If the policy is less than 30 days old, even a small property claim goes to investigation. That rule is not in the SOP. Maria and Devon know it.

Interviewer: What happens when the policy number is missing?

Claims lead: The coordinator searches by name in the policy system. Duplicate names are common, so they message the broker in Slack. Nothing records that wait except the Slack thread.

Interviewer: Who approves payment?

Claims lead: An adjuster recommends it. Claims over $25,000 require a manager. Suspected fraud always goes to investigation regardless of amount.

Interviewer: What is the current pain?

Claims lead: We do not have a measured baseline yet. People complain about queue time, but I cannot give you a reliable number.
